    • Biomimetic Prosthetics: Advancing Actuation and Biocompatibility

    Biomimetic prosthetics are designed to mimic the natural movements and functions of human limbs. This means that they can move more like real limbs, giving users greater control and a more natural experience. They are also made from materials that are compatible with the body, reducing the risk of rejection or discomfort. This combination of natural movement and biocompatibility makes biomimetic prosthetics a promising option for improving the lives of amputees.
